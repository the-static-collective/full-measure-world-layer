# GRACE-MERCY-BROKEN-PROMISE-001

This is the first executable specimen of the Grace ⇄ Mercy dual-sheet contract.

**Source mode:** fixed fictional fixture. The runtime is not inferring hostility from production participation data and is not evaluating a real person's relationship.

## Fixture facts

- `promise_made` — KNOWN
- `promise_missed_1` — KNOWN
- `promise_missed_2` — KNOWN
- `consequence_absorbed_by_other` — KNOWN
- `warning_status_contested` — CONTESTED
- `intent_unknown` — UNKNOWN

The first four facts are sufficient for this fixture to establish that a rupture occurred. The fixture does not infer motive.

## Main path

```text
GRACE
  ↓
HELP THEM SUCCEED
  ↓
NOTICE_RUPTURE
  ↓
MERCY AVAILABLE
  ↓
TURN_TO_MERCY
  ↓
NAME_OCCURRENCE
  ↓
TEST_PATTERN
  ↓
WITNESS_CONSEQUENCE
  ↓
BOUND_COMMITMENT
  ↓
DISCERN_REFUSE
  ↓
CLOSE_ENCOUNTER
```

The successful REFUSE path means only: **do not renew this exact commitment under the same conditions**.

It does not label the other person a permanent enemy and does not erase the relationship's history.

## HOLD

`DISCERN_HOLD` is also a valid close. It yields an unresolved disposition rather than failure.

## Returning to Grace

A player may turn back to Grace after discernment. The Mercy boundary remains active until some later attributable occurrence changes it. The sheet flip itself cannot reopen the door.

## Receipt

The deterministic fixture closes with a `MercyEncounterReceipt` containing evidence references, unknowns, the sheet turn, boundary, discernment, disposition, event lineage, and explicit non-claims.
