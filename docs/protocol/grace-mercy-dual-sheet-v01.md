# Grace / Mercy Dual Sheet v0.1

Grace and Mercy are two projections of one particular character. A sheet turn changes the available action grammar; it does not create a second identity, clear history, duplicate inventory, revoke consequences, or reset boundaries.

## Shared body

Both sides share identity, history, relationships, capacity, cards, inventory, commitments, open threads, consequences, and active boundaries.

## Grace

Grace asks: **What good can live here?**

Its vocabulary is generative: welcome, encourage, give, connect, plant, feed, play, pray, build, invite, teach, receive, bless, celebrate, pour.

Grace is not a score for virtue. Full Measure's existing factual measures remain independent of this projection.

## Mercy

Mercy asks: **What actually happened, what must remain protected, and what lawful relationship is still possible now?**

Its vocabulary is discernment-oriented: notice, name, test, hold, witness, bound, interrupt, refuse, separate, repair, restitute, reconcile, release, return.

Mercy never requires certainty about motive before responding to established effects or present risk.

## Evidence states

The contract preserves `KNOWN`, `REPORTED`, `DERIVED`, `CONTESTED`, and `UNKNOWN`. Unknown motive does not make known consequences unknown.

## Y / HOLD / REFUSE

`Y` admits one scoped proposed crossing under present conditions. It is not universal approval.

`HOLD` preserves an unresolved relation without forcing a premature verdict. HOLD is a successful discernment result.

`REFUSE` rejects one proposed crossing. It does not delete a person, carrier, prior relationship, or every future possibility.

## Boundaries survive the flip

Returning from Mercy to Grace does not forgive, reconcile, restore access, restore a role, erase a consequence, or clear an active boundary. Any of those changes requires its own attributable occurrence.

## Authority boundary

A Mercy encounter receipt proves only the bounded event history encoded by the local runtime. It does not establish motive, diagnosis, moral worth, divine interpretation, restored relationship, or removed consequence.
