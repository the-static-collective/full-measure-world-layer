Seven Days
of Returning.

Not seven days to fix your life. Seven opportunities to discover what remains possible—and choose what to care for next.

No account. No streaks. No scores. No demand to share your story. Return at your own pace.

Your privacy comes first. This page asks for no names, stories, contact details, or evidence. It saves only coarse check-ins on this browser if storage is available. No check-ins are sent to any server. A shared or monitored device may expose visits and saved progress; a web host and network can see request metadata. You can clear local check-ins below.

Some participants may choose to help design or review a safe community project. This is voluntary and needs human permission and safeguarding; it is never compensation for separation from a child or a substitute for that relationship.
